NHS RTT Waiting Times

Referral-to-Treatment waiting times across 477 NHS providers, April–July 2025, aggregated from 77M+ raw records down to a clean analytical dataset.
Data-quality finding: 77.8% of patient records fall into an "Other" wait-band category that the source data's band labels couldn't cleanly classify into the standard 0–18 / 18–52 / 52+ week buckets — a data-quality issue significant enough to flag before drawing conclusions from the "on target" figures alone.
